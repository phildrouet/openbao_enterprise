Scoped by the plugins you run, nothing else

We count plugin types

Every OpenBao auth method, secrets engine and database integration is a plugin. Your contract is scoped around the distinct plugin types you actually use, and only those.

Not clusters. Not identities.

Eight clusters running Kubernetes auth, K/V, PKI and PostgreSQL is four plugins. Growth in clusters, clients or consumers does not grow the contract.

Core set as standard

The thirteen widely-used auth, secrets and database plugins are supported as standard. Non-core and community plugins can be brought into scope; everything under contract gets the same support levels.

Your subscription funds the roadmap

Every known gap against Vault Enterprise has a funded development track, with accepted RFCs and target releases. Support revenue is what pays for the engineering, so the cover you buy also closes the gaps you care about.

Disaster recovery and horizontal scale

PostgreSQL-backed storage for horizontal scalability and disaster recovery. RFC accepted and in development, targeting v2.7.0 in 2026.
